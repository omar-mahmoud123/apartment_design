# New Cairo flat

Furnished layout for our New Cairo apartment (family of six).

- **3D walkthrough:** open `index.html` (or the GitHub Pages link). Walk room to room at eye height, or switch to the dollhouse view. Works on phone and computer.
- **Plan and room guide:** [`Furnished-Plan-and-Room-Guide.pdf`](Furnished-Plan-and-Room-Guide.pdf): page 1 is the furnished plan, page 2 describes every room, including AC positions and sizes.
- **Furniture and room dimensions:** [`Furniture-and-Room-Dimensions.pdf`](Furniture-and-Room-Dimensions.pdf): overall and room sizes, then one page per room with every piece numbered and its width, depth, height and position.
- **Plan image:** [`Furniture-Plan.png`](Furniture-Plan.png): full-resolution furnished floor plan.

All furniture is drawn to real sizes, checked against the dimensioned plan. Ceilings are 3.25 m.
